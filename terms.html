Terms of Use

Picked Fight, the fight picks game · Effective 6 October 2026

The short version. Picked Fight is a free picks game between friends. You must be 17 or older to play. Be rough on each other's picks, not on who people are: no slurs, no threats, no sexual content about real people, no spam. There is zero tolerance for abusive content or abusive players: anything can be reported, anyone can be blocked, and content or accounts that break these rules are removed. There is no wagering in the app.

Agreeing to these terms

By signing in with your email, you agree to these terms and to the Privacy Policy. If you don't agree, you can still browse fight cards, but don't sign in or make picks.

Who can play

You must be at least 17 years old. The app shows betting odds and uncensored jokes between players, so it is meant for adults. One person per account, please; don't sign in as someone else or pick a nickname that impersonates another player or a real person.

The game

Picks, points, the leaderboard, the belt, challenges and rooms are for fun. Nothing in the app is a bet, nothing costs money and nothing pays out. Odds are shown for information only, and the app doesn't take, place or broker wagers. Any "stake" written on a challenge is between the players and is not handled or enforced by Picked Fight. Scores follow the rules shown in the app (Ranks → ℹ). Results come from public sources and are occasionally corrected after a card, which can change scores.

What you post

Nicknames, room names, roasts, challenges and nudges are written by players. You are responsible for what you send. These are not allowed:

  • slurs, or attacks on anyone for their race, ethnicity, religion, sex, gender, sexual orientation or disability
  • threats of violence, or encouraging anyone to harm themselves or others
  • sexual content about real people, and any sexual content involving minors
  • harassing a player who has asked you to stop, or getting around a block
  • someone else's personal information (address, phone number, workplace and the like)
  • spam, advertising, or anything illegal

Trash Talk roasts are written by an AI from what you type, and you choose whether to send them. Read a roast before you send it: once you tap send, it's yours.

Reporting and blocking

Anything another player sends you has a 🚩 Report button, and you can 🚫 Block anyone from a roast, a challenge or their row on the leaderboard. A blocked player can't send you roasts, challenges or nudges, and isn't told you blocked them. Manage blocks under Menu → Privacy & Safety.

The admin reviews reports, aiming to act within 24 hours. Content that breaks these rules is removed, and a player who posts it can have their account suspended or deleted without warning.

AI features

Breakdowns, Ask Claude, Parlay Picks, the scouting report, FightBot and Trash Talk are written by AI models. They can be wrong. Don't rely on them for betting or any other decision, and check anything important against an official source.
