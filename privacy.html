Privacy Policy

Picked Fight, the fight picks game · Effective 4 October 2026

The short version. Picked Fight is a picks game for a group of friends. We keep what the game needs to work: your picks, the name you choose, and your notification settings. Your name is visible to the other players, and so are your picks once each fight locks, because that's the leaderboard. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't use tracking cookies or third-party analytics.

Some settings (your theme, which cards you've expanded, photos already looked up) are stored only on your device, in your browser's local storage, and never leave it.

AI features

When you tap an AI feature (⚡ AI, 💬 Ask Claude, 🎰 Parlay Picks, the scouting report, FightBot, or Trash Talk), the text needed for that request is sent to an AI provider to write the answer: the fight data, what you typed, and for FightBot or a roast, the relevant players' nicknames, picks and scores. Trash Talk roasts are written by xAI's Grok; everything else is written by Anthropic's Claude. We don't send your email address or account ID to either. Nothing is sent unless you tap the feature, and the first time you do, the app asks before sending anything. You can turn AI features off again under Menu → Privacy & Safety.

Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent personal data, and we don't share it for advertising. It is processed by the services that run the app, only to run it:

  • Supabase: our database, sign-in and server functions.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the app's files. Like any web host, it sees your IP address when your device loads the app.
  • Anthropic and xAI: the AI features above, only when you use them.
  • Your browser's push service (Apple, Google or Mozilla): delivers notifications you've turned on.
  • Wikipedia / Wikimedia: your device fetches fighter photos and bios directly from Wikipedia. No account information is included.
  • ESPN: your device loads UFC fighters' headshots directly from ESPN's image server. No account information is included.

We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Other players' content

Roasts, challenges, nudges, nicknames and room names are written by other players. Anything another player sends you has a 🚩 Report button, and you can 🚫 Block anyone: you stop getting their roasts, challenges and nudges, and they can't challenge you. The admin reviews reports and removes content (or accounts) that break the rules: no slurs, no threats, no sexual content about real people, no spam. Manage blocked players in the app under Menu → Privacy & Safety.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Ranks → ✏️ Profile and choose Delete my account. That permanently deletes your account straight away: your sign-in and linked email address, your nickname, your picks, challenges you were part of, the rooms you own, your seats in other rooms, your blocks, roasts sent to you, your notification subscription and settings, and your AI usage counts. Two things are kept: reports (a moderation record, whoever filed them), and the per-network AI limit counts, which are tied to an IP address rather than your account and expire after 30 days. If you can't open the app, contact us (below) and we'll delete it for you.

Children

Picked Fight is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. It shows betting odds and uncensored jokes between friends, so it is meant for adults. If you think a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

Data travels over HTTPS. Database access rules let you change only your own picks, settings and blocks. Server keys are kept on the server, never in the app. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect what we hold.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. Significant changes will also be announced in the app's What's New.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/andyrbrett/ufc-dashboard/issues. For a deletion request, your in-app nickname is all we need, and it is already public on the leaderboard: don't post your email address or other personal details there. Inside the app, Delete my account (above) does it straight away, and 🚩 Report reaches only the admin.
