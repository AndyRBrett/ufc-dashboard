How Points Work
Pick the winner1 pt
Correct finish method+0.5 pt
Underdog +150 to +249+0.5 pt
Underdog +250 or longer+1 pt
Bonus pick (POTN/FOTN)+1 pt
Methods: KO/TKO · Submission · Decision
Underdog bonuses only pay on a correct pick, and use the moneyline shown on the card (the closing line once the fight is done). Anything under +150 is priced as a coin flip, so it scores as a normal pick. A bout with no line on record pays no bonus.
🏆 The Belt
Top scorer of each card takes the belt. The champ defends every event — skip a card and anyone who scores can steal it. A challenger has to beat the champ's score, not match it: a tie involving the champ is a successful defence.

If the champ is out of it and challengers finish level, the belt goes to the highest career accuracy (the % on your row), then to whoever has made the most picks. If two are identical on both, the belt is vacated. Tap Title History at the bottom of the board for the full lineage — a belt won on a tiebreak says so.
⚔️ Challenges
Tap ⚔️ on a rival's row to call them out on one fight or the whole card, with stakes (wheel spin, $5, or your own). They accept or decline; results settle automatically when fights finish. No pick on the contested fight = forfeit. Tie = push, nobody pays. Losers of wheel stakes get a "spin now" button in the inbox.
⏰ Nudges
Friends who haven't finished their main-card picks for the next card show under "Next up" with their progress (e.g. 3/6). Tap a name to send them a callout push — it's signed with your name. Max 3 nudges per person per day, resets at midnight.
⚡ Activity
The Activity strip on the home screen shows pick lock-ins, hot streaks, belt changes, and challenges as they happen — spoiler-free.
