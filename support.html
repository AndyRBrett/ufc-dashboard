Support

Picked Fight, the fight picks game

Need help? Most questions are answered below, and the app's own ⋯ More → FightBot Help can explain any screen. For anything else, contact us.

Signing in

Anyone can browse cards. To make picks, tap a fighter and sign in with your email: we send a one-time code, with no password to remember. Signing in on a new phone with the same email brings your picks and stats back. If the code doesn't arrive, check your spam folder and tap Resend code.

Picks and scoring

A winner is 1 point, the right method adds +0.5, an underdog win adds a bonus, and each card has two 🔒 locks worth +1 if right and −1 if wrong. The full rules are under Ranks → ℹ. Each fight locks shortly before it starts: the first fight of each segment at that segment's start time, and every later fight once the fight before it ends. Other players' picks become visible only once that fight locks.

Notifications

Turn on the 🔔 bell for fight reminders and results. On iPhone, notifications need the app added to your Home Screen first (Share → Add to Home Screen), then open it from there and tap the bell.

Reporting and blocking

Use 🚩 Report or 🚫 Block on a roast, a challenge, or a player's row in Ranks. Manage blocks under Menu → Privacy & Safety. The rules are in the Terms of Use.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Ranks → ✏️ Profile → Delete my account. It happens straight away; the Privacy Policy lists exactly what it removes. If you can't open the app, contact us below with your nickname and we'll delete it for you.

Contact

Open an issue at github.com/andyrbrett/ufc-dashboard/issues. Your in-app nickname is all we need, and it is already public on the leaderboard: don't post your email address or other personal details there. To report something a player sent you, use 🚩 Report in the app instead: it reaches only the admin.
